# Circular-orbit reference

For a point mass in a circular orbit around a spherical Earth:

v_c = sqrt(μ / r), where r = R + h.

At h = 400,000 m:

- r = 6,771,000 m
- v_c ≈ 7,672.59 m/s
- period ≈ 5,544.86 s, or 92.41 min

These are project calculations derived from the cited equation and constants, not flight predictions.
