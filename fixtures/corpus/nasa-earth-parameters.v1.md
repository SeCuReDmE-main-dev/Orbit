# Earth parameters used by the teaching model

Orbit Companion v1 uses the volumetric mean radius, 6,371,000 m, for its simplified spherical Earth.

- μ = 3.98600 × 10^14 m³/s²
- R_mean = 6.37100 × 10^6 m
- R_equatorial = 6.378137 × 10^6 m

The simulation must state which radius it uses. Different radius definitions are not automatically contradictions.
