# Orbit intuition

An orbit combines sideways motion with continuous gravitational acceleration toward Earth. Too little tangential speed can intersect Earth. More speed changes the trajectory; sufficiently high speed can produce escape. The laboratory separates this intuition from its numerical approximation.
