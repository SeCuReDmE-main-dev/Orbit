## Design brief (condensed)

Keep the recognizable feminine Synthia identity, sage eyes, ivory segmented ceramic, silver mechanics and a restrained teal holographic edge. Restore believable adult facial proportions, an attentive expression, integrated shoulders and refined neck mechanisms. Avoid an elongated egg-shaped head, glassy doll eyes, detached sphere shoulders, a rectangular portrait frame or a generic wireframe robot. Show a dominant three-quarter portrait with secondary front and profile views on dark graphite.

## Translation to actual 3D

The existing Blender studies are preserved separately. They do not yet meet this art direction and are not approved for release. The concept image is not substituted for a rotatable mesh. The face/silhouette review remains a required gate before full integration. The renderer must remain portable, with no account, conversation or service authority.

No production Education or Synthia assets were altered.
