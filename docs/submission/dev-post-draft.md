## What I Built

Orbit Companion is a Chrome side-panel companion that turns a NASA page and a focused question into a bounded research mission. It preserves sources, units, contradictions, decisions and limits. A small orbital laboratory asks the learner to predict, observe and explain a two-body result.

The core question is: can structured content make an agent more honest and useful than a flat search result?

## How I Used Sanity

Sources are separate from claims. Claims preserve units, qualifiers, authority and access date. This matters for Earth radius: mean and equatorial radii are both valid but not interchangeable without their definitions.

## Simulation boundary

The model uses a fixed spherical Earth, a point satellite and Newtonian gravity. The 3D view is illustrative; numerical state is authoritative. It does not model drag, thrust, oblateness or operational constraints.
