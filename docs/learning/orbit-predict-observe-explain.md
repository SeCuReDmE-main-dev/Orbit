# Lesson — Predict, observe, explain an orbit

Duration: 20–30 minutes. Status: prepared, not yet assessed.

## Model

A point satellite moves around a fixed spherical Earth under Newtonian gravity.

- μ = 3.98600 × 10^14 m³/s²
- R = 6.37100 × 10^6 m
- acceleration a = -μr / |r|³
- circular speed v_c = sqrt(μ / |r|)

Excluded: atmosphere, drag, thrust, Earth rotation, oblateness, Moon, Sun and relativistic effects. Visual sizes are illustrative.

At altitude 400 km:

- distance from center: 6,771 km
- circular speed: approximately 7.673 km/s
- period: approximately 92.41 min

## Predict

Before running, predict the paths at 100%, 90% and 110% of circular speed and state what observation would change the explanation.

## Observe

Record closest altitude, farthest altitude, simulated duration, collision or escape state, energy-drift metric and whether reduced-motion mode was used.

## Explain

Explain why gravity uses distance from Earth’s center, which values come from sources versus calculations, and what additional evidence would be needed for flight-quality use.

## Pass

The learner distinguishes sourced constants, derived calculations, rendered scale and assumptions, then applies the explanation to an unseen speed.
